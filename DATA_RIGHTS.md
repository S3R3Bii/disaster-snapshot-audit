# Source rights, units and limits

The current official [OpenFEMA Terms and Conditions](https://www.fema.gov/about/openfema/terms-conditions) were retrieved 31 October 2026 UTC and permit statistical research/reporting with required attribution. They prohibit reidentification, publishing facts that identify individuals, and using these data to determine individual rights or benefit eligibility. This study uses Public Assistance project data for a county-aggregation/availability audit, not individual-assistance data or benefit decisions.

Native project records and organization identifiers remain private. Public data contain county/cutoff sufficient aggregates and necessary source metadata only. No individual linkage, names, contact information, project narratives, addresses, FEMA/DHS seals or agency endorsement. Code is MIT; the data are attributed to OpenFEMA and subject to its terms, not relicensed as code.

Dataset: [PublicAssistanceFundedProjectsDetails v 2](https://www.fema.gov/api/open/v 2/PublicAssistanceFundedProjectsDetails).
[Dictionary](https://www.fema.gov/openfema-data-page/public-assistance-funded-projects-details-v 2) and [API definitions](https://www.fema.gov/about/openfema/api) were captured 31 October 2026 UTC; exact times and SHA-256 values are retained in source manifests.

FEMA describes these financial data as derived from Grants Manager rather than its official financial systems and not intended for official federal reporting. The records exclude projects still in pre-obligation processing and include migration-related population changes. County identifies applicant location, not necessarily damaged work. federalShareObligated is reported grant funding available to the grantee, not actual payments.

Required notice:

This product uses the Federal Emergency Management Agency’s OpenFEMA API, but is not endorsed by FEMA. The Federal Government or FEMA cannot vouch for the data or analyses derived from these data after the data have been retrieved from the Agency's website(s).
